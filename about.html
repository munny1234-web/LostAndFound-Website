About Lost&Found

Lost&Found is the web companion to the LostAndFound Android app, sharing one live database. Post on your phone, manage claims on the web; everything stays in sync in real time.

It combines on-device AI (image labeling and text recognition), semantic claim verification, live maps and secure chat to do one thing well: get lost things back to their owners.

Built as a Final Year Project by Jannatul Ferdous Munny · Dept. of CSE, ADUST.
