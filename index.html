How it works

Three steps between losing something and getting it back.

📸

1 · Post with a photo

Our AI reads text on the item (serials, name tags) and detects what it is — automatically, right in your browser.

🤝

2 · Get matched

Photo Search and semantic matching compare every lost report against every found report and rank the likely pairs.

🛡️

3 · Verify & reunite

Finders set a secret verification question. Claims are AI-scored for authenticity before anything is handed over.

Built for trust

AI claim verification

Claim answers are compared against the finder's hidden detail with a sentence-similarity model — fraud gets flagged before it happens.

💬

Arrange handovers without sharing your phone number until you choose to.
